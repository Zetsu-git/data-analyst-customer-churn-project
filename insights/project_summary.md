# Project Summary

## Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze customer churn for a subscription business and identify patterns that may explain why customers leave.

## What Churn Means

Churn means a customer stopped using or paying for a product. A high churn rate can hurt a subscription business because the company must replace lost customers just to keep revenue stable.

## Analysis Approach

1. Counted total customers and churned customers.
2. Calculated the overall churn rate.
3. Compared churn rate across subscription plans.
4. Compared support ticket volume between churned and retained customers.
5. Compared customer tenure and inactivity between churned and retained customers.
6. Identified customers who may be at risk of churning.

## Early Findings

- Customers with more support tickets appear more likely to churn.
- Customers who have not logged in recently appear more likely to churn.
- Enterprise customers may need closer review because their monthly price is higher and churn can have a larger revenue impact.

## Recommendations

- Monitor customers with high support ticket counts.
- Create a retention campaign for inactive customers.
- Review common support issues to reduce customer frustration.
- Track churn by plan every month.
- Contact high-value customers before they become inactive for too long.
